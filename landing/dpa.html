Data processing agreement

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This agreement covers the personal data Cat Tan Operations Ltd handles when you buy a TanCat licence or email us. It does not cover your test data, because we never receive it.

Who we are. TanCat is a product of Cat Tan Operations Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17576111). Registered office: Flat 2, Bournehall, 2 Bourne Close, Bournemouth, BH2 6BW. Contact: hello@tancat.dev. The privacy notice describes the website; this page describes the seller's side.

1. Who is the controller

For everything TanCat generates or stores while you use it - your stories, your target pages, the generated tests and the evidence - you are the controller and we are not a processor, because the software runs on your own machine and sends us nothing. The egress audit lists every outbound call the product can make: your LLM endpoint and your target site.

That is the whole list. There is no customer account, no telemetry, and no analytics.

3. Who else is involved

  • Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record. It takes the payment and handles tax, so we never see your card details.
  • Zoho is our email provider. It stores the mailbox behind hello@tancat.dev, and its mailbox data is held in its EU data centre.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and keeps request logs, as the privacy notice says.

4. What we do with it

  • We use it only to sell and support the licence, and to meet tax and accounting duties.
  • We keep it for the periods the privacy notice states: purchase records for the licence term and then six years, and support email for up to 24 months after the last reply.
  • We answer a request to see, correct, export or delete it within one month.
  • We tell you without undue delay if a breach affects it.
  • We do not sell it, and we share it only with the providers above, or where the law requires.

5. International transfers

Where personal data leaves the UK, the transfer relies on the UK addendum to the standard contractual clauses, or on an adequacy decision. The privacy notice has the detail.

6. Ending this agreement

This agreement ends when we no longer hold your personal data. Ask us to delete it and we will, except for the purchase records tax law requires us to keep.

7. Contact and complaints

Write to hello@tancat.dev. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Privacy notice

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This notice explains what personal data tancat.dev collects, why, and what rights you have. It covers the website only. The TanCat software runs inside your network: it sends data to your LLM endpoint and your target site, and to nowhere else. See the egress audit.

Who is responsible. TanCat (the controller) decides why and how personal data on this website is used. Contact: hello@tancat.dev.

What this website collects

WhatWhenWhy
Your email address and the content of your messageOnly if you email usTo answer you and, if you ask, to issue a license
IP address and standard request logsEvery visitKept by our host (GitHub Pages) for security and abuse prevention

There are no forms, no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising trackers on this site. We do not profile you and we make no automated decisions about you.

Who else processes your data

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and keeps request logs. GitHub is a US company; its parent, Microsoft, applies the EU/UK standard contractual clauses.
  • jsDelivr serves the Tailwind CSS file, and Google Fonts serves the page fonts. Both see the IP address of every visitor as a normal part of serving those files.
  • Our payment provider (the merchant of record) sells the paid licenses and processes payment data. It is the seller of record, so we never see your card details.
  • Our email provider stores the mailbox behind hello@tancat.dev.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires it.

International transfers

Some of the providers above are in the United States. Where personal data leaves the UK, the transfer relies on the UK addendum to the standard contractual clauses, or on an adequacy decision.

How long we keep it

  • Email inquiries: up to 24 months after the last reply.
  • License and invoice records: for the license term, then as long as tax law requires (currently 6 years).
  • Host request logs: set by GitHub, not by us.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to processing, or ask us to restrict it. Write to hello@tancat.dev. We answer within one month.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted on this page.
